The structure

  • Every bot has a clear role, home and owner, as in any well run business.
  • Any bot can call any bot directly; there are no reporting gates.

How work gets done

  • Lee picks where work starts. Work is tackled the way it needs to be, not the way the chart is drawn.
  • Any bot can call CTO, Senior Developer or any colleague or shared skill directly when it genuinely helps. A deal review in Deal Desk, for example, may pull in CFO, Risk & Compliance Officer and Senior Developer as it grows.
  • When a job grows, a bot suggests who else to bring in and why, and goes ahead on Lee's yes.
  • Teams form around the work and stand down when it is done.

Hand-offs

  • Where Lee starts is where he finishes: the bot he starts with gives him one joined up answer in that chat.
  • The bot that hands work out stays the owner: it chases until the work is done and brings any questions or blockers to Lee itself.
  • The bot given the work reports back to the bot that asked, not to Lee or Non Stop.

What stays fixed

Each job has one owner and one joined up answer. One guardrail: Non Stop is the only writer of Notion Notes.

Routines in the background

The Collabor8 Routine bots, such as Scout, MNO Research, Micro Insurance and Boardy Rolodex, with Night Prospects running overnight, work in the background to fast track growth work and ongoing research for Collabor8 and Lee. What they find feeds the whole team: any bot can pick it up and take it forward.
