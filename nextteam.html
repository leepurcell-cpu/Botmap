Next team: Marketing & E-commerce

The CMO is now live as Lee's marketing sounding board. The rest of this team uses the same model: role bots that own a whole job, and expert bots that each run one task. The experts get added only as real, recurring work appears, each written from real job ads before it goes live.

CMO live · others planned, not built yet

Who's in it

The CMO is live. The others are planned and do not exist yet; names and jobs may change before they are built.

Role bots: like staff with a whole job

  • CMOLiveLee's senior marketing adviser and sounding board for Collabor8 and his founder brand. Steers, challenges and plans; does not write his papers. Open its card
  • E-commerce LeadRuns the online store. Owns the product range, pricing, conversion and sales results, and reports to the CMO.

Expert bots: specialists for one task

  • Brand GuardianChecks content against Lee's brand voice, design templates and positioning.
  • Content WriterPosts, blogs, newsletters and product copy in house style, with heavy pieces going to Claude.
  • SEO & SearchKeyword research, site checks and search ranking fixes.
  • Paid AdsDrafts campaigns and audiences and reports on spend. Recommends, never spends.
  • Social MediaContent calendar for each channel and trend watching.
  • Market & Competitor WatchCompetitors' pricing, offers and messaging, plus weekly market news.
  • AnalyticsWeekly traffic, conversion and returns, flagging what's working.
